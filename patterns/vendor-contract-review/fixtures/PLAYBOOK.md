# Demo negotiation playbook

Replace this file with your organization's positions before anyone relies on the flags. These values are **illustrative** for the Crafting demo.

## Limitation of liability

- Standard: mutual cap at 12 months of fees paid or payable in the prior 12 months
- Acceptable: 6–24 months of fees
- Escalation: uncapped liability, or consequential damages included in direct damages

## Indemnification

- Standard: mutual indemnification for IP infringement and data breach
- Acceptable: third-party claims only
- Escalation: unilateral indemnification, uncapped indemnification

## IP ownership

- Standard: each party keeps pre-existing IP; customer owns customer data
- Escalation: broad assignment of customer pre-existing IP, work-for-hire on customer materials we did not pay to create

## Data protection

- Standard: DPA required for any personal data; sub-processor notice; deletion on termination; breach notice within 72 hours
- Escalation: no DPA, cross-border transfer with no mechanism, unlimited vendor use of customer data

## Term and termination

- Standard: annual term, 30-day termination for convenience
- Acceptable: multi-year with convenience termination after the initial term
- Escalation: auto-renewal without notice, no termination for convenience

## Governing law

- Preferred: customer's principal place of business
- Acceptable: New York, Delaware, California, England and Wales
- Escalation: exclusive venue that is impractical for the customer, mandatory arbitration with no injunctive relief carve-out
